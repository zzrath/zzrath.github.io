Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

Crosspost is a personal app used only by its owner (zzrath) to publish their own videos to their own YouTube, Instagram and TikTok accounts.

  • What it accesses: only the owner's own accounts, after the owner logs in and approves access. It uses basic profile info, the ability to upload/publish videos, and the owner's own video statistics and comments.
  • Where data is stored: login tokens, settings and post history are stored only on the owner's computer. Nothing is sent to any server other than the platforms themselves.
  • Sharing: no data is sold or shared with anyone.
  • Deleting data: the owner can disconnect any account in the app or revoke access in that platform's settings at any time, and delete the app's local data folder.
